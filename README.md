# project-4292ab11
Online tee-time booking for Green Lotus Golf Club, an 18-hole course near Ho Chi Minh City. The home page shows the course and today's open tee times (every 10 minutes from 5:30, with the green fee per player for weekday and weekend). Golfers sign in to book: a tee-time detail page lets them pick the date, the time, 9 or 18 holes and 1 to 4 players
